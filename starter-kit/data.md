## Listen | Individual · 6 minutes
> Listen and complete the text with the words you hear.

[play audio.mp3 00:10 "The audio"]

You can {replace} the audio file with your own. Go to the {folder} and paste your own audio.mp3 there. You can have AI {transcribe} it for you. You can even open data.md and change the true/false sentences by {hand}. If you change the {final} T for an F, the sentence turns from true to false. You can also change what {part} of the audio you play, or use the {prompt} in the AI folder to have AI do all of that for you.
