LessonMark prompts – from a YouTube video to a ready lesson

1. Open 1-gemini-extract-video-script.txt, fill in the TEACHER'S CHOICES at the top,
   and paste the whole text into Gemini. It returns the timestamped script, clips,
   vocabulary, grammar and discussion ideas.

2. Open 2-claude-build-lessonmark-lesson.txt, fill in the TEACHER'S CHOICES, paste
   Gemini's answer at the bottom, and send it to Claude (with the lessonmark-lesson
   skill installed). Claude returns data.md.

3. Put data.md next to LessonMark's index.html.
