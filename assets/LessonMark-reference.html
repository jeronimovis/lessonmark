Most used copy, paste, change the words

These six cover most lessons. Each one is complete: paste it into data.md and it works.

The header

The first lines of the file, between two --- lines. Only title is required.

---
title: Make This Lesson Your Own
level: B1
intro: A short demo lesson.
style: classic
---

An exercise

## starts a numbered exercise. The line after it, starting with >, tells students what to do.

## Watch | Individual · 4 minutes
> Watch the video. Are the sentences true or false?

True or false

The letter at the end is the answer: [t] true, [f] false.

[tf] You can use your own audio files. [t]
[tf] YouTube plays from your computer. [f]

A clip

File or YouTube link, start time, end time, name. Times are minutes:seconds.

[play audio.mp3 00:10 00:40 "Part 1"]
[play https://youtu.be/p2hnrfN58H4 01:26 03:24 "Clip B"]

Gaps

Curly brackets make a gap. Use | for other accepted answers.

Go to the {folder} and paste your own {audio file|audio} there.

Multiple choice and discussion

* marks the right option. Discussion questions have no answer.

[m] What can you replace?
- Only the video
* The audio and the video
- Only the exercises
[/m]

[discuss] What would you change first? Why?

General rules

  1. Tags go at the start of a line, in lowercase: [tf], [m], [play …].
  2. Block tags open and close on their own lines: [m] … [/m].
  3. One-line tags keep the whole item on one line and have no closing tag: [tf], [short], [odd], [discuss], [play], [img], [timer], [link].
  4. Inside a tag, titles, captions and names go in straight quotes: "Clip A". Curly quotes break the tag.
  5. Curly brackets always make a gap. To show the symbols themselves, write \{ \}.
  6. * marks the correct option · = introduces an answer · | separates accepted alternatives.
  7. Numbers, letters and the answer key are added by the page. Leave them out of the file.
  8. Times: mm:ss or hh:mm:ss, digits only.

The full rules for AI assistants are in the LessonMark specification.

Setup files and loading

The folder

  • index.html: the page. It stays the same for every lesson.
  • data.md: the lesson, in plain text (UTF-8).
  • Any media the lesson uses, in the same folder: audio.mp3, video.mp4, image01.png…
  • To put a lesson online, upload the whole folder.

How a lesson opens

  • The page looks for data.md next to it, then data.txt.
  • Another file: index.html?lesson=venice.md
  • If it finds nothing, it shows Open a lesson: drag a file in, choose one, or paste the lesson text.
  • LessonMark online always starts on that screen. It's the place for YouTube lessons.
  • Open in the top bar loads another lesson at any time.

Pasting from an AI

  • Paste the AI's whole answer if that's easier. The page keeps the lesson and drops the chat text around it.
  • Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) shows the lesson.
  • A pasted lesson lives in that browser tab. To keep it, save it as data.md.

Opened from your computer

  • Chrome and Edge ask once for the lesson's folder and then remember it.
  • Other browsers ask you to choose the file each time.
  • Audio, video and images work. YouTube clips need the page online.

What happens automatically

Numbering and navigation

  • Every ## exercise gets a number and a place in the sticky top menu.
  • Items and gaps are numbered inside each exercise.
  • Matching, ordering and categorising are shuffled, and never start in the right order.

Checking

  • Check answers marks each answer green or red and shows a score.
  • Show answers fills in every answer in blue. Clear starts again.
  • Checking ignores capitals, accents, curly or straight apostrophes, extra spaces and final punctuation.

Help and answer key

  • Need help? Word bank: a folded list of the answers under exercises with typed gaps or short answers.
  • Answer key: folded at the end of the page, grouped by exercise.

Safety nets

  • Mistakes in the lesson file appear in a red box with their line numbers.
  • A missing image shows “Cannot find … in this folder”. A missing recording shows a red message in its player.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
The “Open a lesson” screenOn LessonMark online, that's where you start. Anywhere else, the page found no data.md or data.txt next to it. Check the name (capitals count), or choose the file.
A red box at the topIt lists mistakes with their line numbers. Fix them by hand, or paste the lines back to the AI that wrote the lesson.
Red box: “not closed”A block tag is missing its [/tag] line.
YouTube won't playThe page is opened from your computer. Use LessonMark online, or “Open on YouTube” for now.
A gap appears where it shouldn'tCurly brackets in normal text. Write \{ and \}.
Pasted lesson shows chat textCopy only the lesson box from the AI, starting at the first ---.
Online, data.md can't be found on GitHub PagesAdd an empty file called .nojekyll at the top of the site, so .md files are published as they are.
A transparent PNG shows a boxThe page is opened from your computer. Add transparent to the [img] line.
A black drawing disappears in dark modeUse mid-tone colours in transparent images.
